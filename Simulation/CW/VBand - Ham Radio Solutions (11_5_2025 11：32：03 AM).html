Welcome to VBand, the internet Virtual CW Band.

June 4, 2025 - We have recently made some improvements to VBand.
  • You may need to clear the cache for our site on your browser in order for it to work properly. You can do this on MacOS by going to our website and pressing command-shift-R. If you are on Windows, go to our site and hit Ctrl-F5.
  • CW Hotline can now communicate with any VBand channel. Update to the latest CW Hotline firmware required.
  • Users can create private VBand channels. Create the channel name in the settings tab and share with your friends. Append " (ND)" to the name to make it decode only.
  • VBand transmissions are now displayed more like phone text messages. A pause of 5 seconds will complete the message.
  • Please share any comments on our Discord Server.

Click a channel below to get started.

Warning - We have received numerous reports from users who have purchased clone / counterfeit VBand adapters that are not compatible with our website, even though they claim to be. We do not support these adapters. You can find our supported adapter on the Store tab above.

- Version Apr 19, 2023 This project was created by two friends who wanted to (re)learn CW, but didn't regularly have great propagation between them to practice, and were a bit embarrased to get on the air at their current skill level. While it allows code to be sent with a keyboard as either a paddle or straight key, it really shines using the USB paddle interface available in the Store tab. The goal was to make a fun way to practice sending and receiving CW with a buddy, without worrying about a radio, an antenna, a license, good propagation, or RF noise.
