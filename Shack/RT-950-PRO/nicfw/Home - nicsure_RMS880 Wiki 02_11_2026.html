Factory Reset / Storage Reset

> When the radio is turned off, press and hold:  
  **(RT-880)** `EMG` _(Top Button)_  
  **(RT-950)** `SK3` _(Bottom Side Button)_  
  while turning the radio on.  
> Wait until the display tells you the wipe process is complete.  
> Turn the radio off and then back on.
