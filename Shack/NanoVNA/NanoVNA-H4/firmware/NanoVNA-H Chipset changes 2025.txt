NanoVNA-H rev3.7 and NanoVNA-H4 rev4.4 Replacing NXP SA602A with ZeeTK NE602A.

Firmware synchronization 1.2.43

Due to the discontinuation of NXP SA612A, we started to produce compatible chip ZeeTK NE602A, but due to the different chip manufacturing process, there is a slight difference between the two in the use of harmonics, new compatible ZeeTK NE602A NanoVNA-H rev3.7 and NanoVNA-H4 rev4.4 version hardware schematic. Using the SJWCH5351 (formerly SMC5351 renamed) as a signal source, it can operate down to 600 Hz. New hardware improves port isolation below 300 MHz.
